# Decision 0001: Use Leadership Guides Instead of Job Descriptions

Owner: Vision 2040 PMO

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-07-09

## Context

The Vision 2040 workstream document originally defined job descriptions, accountability frameworks, and KPI scorecards for the nine workstreams.

During review, it became clear that ordinary job descriptions would be too narrow.

Each workstream lead is not merely performing a job.

Each lead is temporarily building an enduring organisational capability for Rhema Nigeria.

## Decision

The repository will develop Leadership Guides for the nine workstream leads rather than conventional job descriptions.

## Rationale

A Leadership Guide can support:

- recruitment
- appointment
- onboarding
- coaching
- performance review
- succession
- knowledge transfer

It can also explain the philosophy of the role, not only its tasks.

## Consequences

Each guide will include sections such as:

- purpose of the role
- why the workstream matters
- strategic context
- philosophy of the workstream
- recruitment profile
- who should not be appointed
- first 90 days
- working relationships
- decision authority
- success indicators
- common mistakes
- legacy
- letter to my successor

This creates more work at the start.

It should produce documents that are more useful over time.

## Affected documents

- `templates/leadership-guide-template.md`
- `guides/leadership-handbook/index.md`
- all workstream leadership guides
