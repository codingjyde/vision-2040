# Capabilities Before Departments

Owner: Vision 2040 PMO

Status: Draft

Version: 1.0.0

## Principle

Rhema Nigeria should define the capabilities required to fulfil Vision 2040 before finalising departments, titles, and reporting lines.

## Meaning

Departments are containers.

Capabilities are what the organisation must actually be able to do.

Vision 2040 requires capabilities such as:

- campus expansion
- leadership formation
- faculty development
- church planting support
- missionary care
- data and reporting
- communications
- publishing
- translation
- financial sustainability
- governance

The organisational structure should serve these capabilities.

## Why it matters

If structure begins with departments, the organisation may protect existing arrangements instead of building what the vision requires.

If structure begins with capabilities, roles and departments can be designed around mission.

## Practical tests

When designing a role or team, ask:

- What capability does this role strengthen?
- What Vision 2040 outcome does it support?
- What decisions should this role make?
- What competencies are required?
- What would break if this capability remains weak?

## Application

This principle is especially important for Workstream 02, Organisation Design & Capability.

It should also shape recruitment, performance scorecards, succession planning, and future restructuring decisions.
