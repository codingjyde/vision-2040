# Build Before Scale

Owner: Vision 2040 PMO

Status: Draft

Version: 1.0.0

## Principle

Rhema Nigeria should build and validate the systems required for expansion before expansion begins.

## Meaning

Growth should not outrun organisational capacity.

Before Rhema Nigeria expands into new campuses, programmes, territories, or initiatives, the required governance, people, finance, training, reporting, operations, communication, and knowledge systems should be in place.

## Why it matters

Premature expansion creates avoidable pressure.

It can weaken standards, confuse authority, strain finances, overload leaders, and dilute Rhema DNA.

Building before scaling allows the organisation to multiply from strength rather than enthusiasm alone.

## Practical tests

Before scaling an initiative, ask:

- Is the operating model documented?
- Has the model been tested?
- Are decision rights clear?
- Are leaders trained?
- Are finances understood?
- Are reporting systems active?
- Are checklists validated?
- Is there a handover package?

## Application

This principle applies to every Vision 2040 workstream.

Each workstream should leave behind a tested system that can support future implementation.
