# Governance Removes Friction

Owner: Vision 2040 PMO

Status: Draft

Version: 1.0.0

## Principle

Good governance should make faithful action easier, not harder.

## Meaning

Governance exists to reduce uncertainty.

It should clarify who decides, who recommends, who must be consulted, and who must be informed.

It should help leaders make good decisions quickly, responsibly, and with confidence.

## What this principle rejects

This principle rejects governance that becomes:

- bureaucracy
- control for its own sake
- unnecessary paperwork
- approval bottlenecks
- unclear committees
- meetings without decisions
- processes nobody uses

## Practical tests

A governance tool is healthy if it:

- reduces confusion
- clarifies authority
- speeds up appropriate decisions
- improves accountability
- preserves trust
- helps leaders work together

A governance tool should be revised if it slows the work without improving decision quality.

## Application

This principle is especially important for Workstream 01, Governance & Leadership.

It also applies to every policy, process, checklist, and review cycle created under Vision 2040.
