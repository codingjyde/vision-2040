# Vision 2040 Workstreams Overview

Owner: Vision 2040 PMO

Status: Draft

Version: 1.0.0

## Purpose

This source document defines the nine Vision 2040 workstreams for the 2026–2027 restructuring phase.

The workstreams are temporary programme structures. They are not permanent departments.

Each workstream exists to design, test, validate, and document an enduring organisational capability before expansion begins from 2028 onwards.

## Restructuring principle

The 2026–2027 phase is not an expansion phase.

Its purpose is preparation.

Rhema Nigeria should build the systems, leadership structures, operating models, governance, reporting, funding, and knowledge base required for sustainable expansion.

The guiding principle is:

> Build before you scale.

## Workstream 01: Governance & Leadership

Design the governance architecture for Vision 2040.

This includes the PMO, decision rights, executive review cycles, escalation protocols, phase-gate approvals, accountability systems, and governance documentation.

The primary question is:

> How will Rhema Nigeria make clear, timely, faithful decisions as the vision grows?

## Workstream 02: Organisation Design & Capability

Design the target organisational architecture for Rhema Nigeria.

This includes roles, reporting lines, capability requirements, competency frameworks, role definitions, succession thinking, hiring readiness, and performance scorecards.

The primary question is:

> What kind of organisation must Rhema Nigeria become to fulfil Vision 2040?

## Workstream 03: Campus Expansion Model

Design the repeatable model for launching and sustaining Rhema campuses.

This includes location selection, staffing, finance, launch checklists, weekly operations, monthly performance reviews, campus maturity, and pilot validation.

The primary question is:

> How can Rhema Nigeria launch healthy campuses without turning every launch into a fresh experiment?

## Workstream 04: Training, Leadership & Formation

Design the full training ecosystem.

This includes curriculum architecture, delivery formats, instructor readiness, faculty development, assessment, certification, leadership formation, and graduate competencies.

The primary question is:

> What kind of leaders must Rhema Nigeria form if Vision 2040 is to become reality?

## Workstream 05: AfterGrad, Missions & Alumni

Design the post-graduation ecosystem.

This includes graduate pathways, church planting support, missionary deployment, alumni engagement, graduate care, placement, impact tracking, and lifelong relationship management.

The primary question is:

> What happens because a person came to Rhema?

## Workstream 06: Finance, Stewardship & Sustainability

Design the financial architecture for Vision 2040.

This includes budget frameworks, financial tracking, diversified funding models, donor reporting, sustainability planning, implementation reserves, and financial readiness for 2028.

The primary question is:

> How will Rhema Nigeria fund a movement faithfully and sustainably for the next fifteen years?

## Workstream 07: Data, Insights & Reporting

Design the measurement and reporting ecosystem.

This includes KPI frameworks, data architecture, dashboards, reporting standards, data quality, learning loops, and executive insight.

The primary question is:

> How will Rhema Nigeria know whether Vision 2040 is truly working?

## Workstream 08: Communications, Brand & Engagement

Design the communication architecture for Vision 2040.

This includes messaging, audience segmentation, internal communication, content pipelines, publishing discipline, stakeholder engagement, storytelling, and crisis communication.

The primary question is:

> How will people understand, trust, carry, and participate in Vision 2040?

## Workstream 09: Publications, Translation & Knowledge Resources

Design the publication and translation infrastructure.

This includes publication priorities, translation workflows, quality assurance, distribution models, knowledge publishing, intellectual property stewardship, and multilingual resource development.

The primary question is:

> How will Rhema Nigeria preserve and multiply the message, wisdom, and resources entrusted to it?

## Cross-workstream expectation

Every workstream should produce:

- a validated operating model
- role clarity
- decision rights
- checklists
- templates
- KPIs
- risk register
- lessons learned
- handover package for 2028

## Handover expectation

By the end of 2027, each workstream should leave behind documents and systems that can survive leadership transition.

The real output is not activity.

The real output is organisational capability.
