Owner: Vision 2040 PMO

## Purpose

This document summarises the KPI framework for the nine Vision 2040 workstreams.

It exists so Leadership Guides can reference one source instead of copying KPI logic repeatedly.

## Source Note

This summary is based on the Vision 2040 Workstream Job Descriptions & KPI Framework, Version 1.0, June 2026.

Verify against the approved PDF before final release.

## Global KPI Weighting Framework

The approved framework weights performance across five categories:

| Category | Weight | Focus |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Execution & Milestones | 30% | Deliverable completion against timeline |
| Quality & Validation | 25% | Checklist validation, accuracy and standards |
| Governance & Compliance | 20% | Protocol adherence and reporting timeliness |
| Collaboration | 15% | Cross-workstream alignment and engagement |
| Continuous Improvement | 10% | Process refinement and feedback integration |
