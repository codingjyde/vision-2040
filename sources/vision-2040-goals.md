# Vision 2040 Goals

Owner: Rhema Nigeria

Status: Extracted source summary

Version: 0.1.0

## Purpose

This document summarises the stated Vision 2040 goals for use across the organisational knowledge repository.

It should be treated as a working source summary until verified against the approved Vision 2040 presentation and any final leadership-approved documents.

## Source Note

This summary is based on the Vision 2040 presentation content already provided to the project.

If any official goal wording changes, update this file first. Other documents should reference this file rather than redefining the goals.

## Goals

1. Ten percent of Nigeria's population will be part of the Rhema family.

2. Establish 40 campuses across Nigeria, including four campuses in Lagos, and at least one Rhema Nigeria campus in every state and the FCT.

3. Train 100,000 leaders who touch every sphere of society, including church, media, government and business.

4. Ten percent of graduates, working with their pastors or denominations, will pioneer 10,000 life-giving churches, mostly in unreached areas in Nigeria and beyond. Ten of Nigeria's largest and most influential churches will be pastored by Rhema graduates.

5. Ten percent of graduates, working with their pastors or denominations, will become cross-cultural missionaries in the least reached areas of Nigeria and beyond, contributing to Nigeria being removed from the list of unreached people groups.

6. Ten percent of graduates will become significantly influential societal leaders who bring change to the nation, resulting in measurable improvement in every area of society. Some will become governors, senators and members of the House of Representatives.

7. Seventy percent of graduates will fulfil vital supportive leadership roles, enabling those called to spiritual and societal leadership to accomplish the vision God has called them to steward.

8. Ten million copies of Faith Library Publications books will be printed and distributed in Nigeria.

9. Fifty of Brother Hagin's and Pastor Hagin's books will be translated into Hausa, with distribution networks established throughout the North. Other key Nigerian language groups will follow.

10. Rhema will be present in tertiary institutions through Kainos, cooperating with campus fellowships to train 500,000 students using RBTC materials across Nigeria.

11. Rhema Nigeria TV and radio programmes will air both timeless Brother Hagin and Pastor Hagin programming, as well as excerpts from current and local Rhema Nigeria activities.

12. Rhema Kids and Youth Academies will cooperate with churches and secondary schools throughout Nigeria to train 500,000 children and youth in leadership using age-appropriate RBTC courses.

13. Provide a strong relational Alumni Association with a supportive, healthy structure that promotes care, communication, connection and accountability.

14. Maintain a strong and healthy Ministerial Association for ordination and licensing of ministers, linking with major governing bodies in Nigeria such as CAN and PFN.

15. Build and/or acquire real estate properties in all states across the country.
