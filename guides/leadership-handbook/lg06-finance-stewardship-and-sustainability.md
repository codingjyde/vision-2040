# Leadership Guide 06: Finance, Stewardship & Sustainability

## Purpose of this Guide

This guide defines the role of the leader responsible for designing the financial architecture for Vision 2040.

## Why This Workstream Exists

Vision 2040 requires more than accounting. It requires financial stewardship, funding confidence and long-term sustainability.

This workstream helps Rhema Nigeria answer one question:

How do we fund a movement for the next fifteen years?

## Mission Statement

Design the financial systems, funding models, reporting structures and stewardship practices required to support Vision 2040 responsibly and sustainably.

## Strategic Context

The 2026-2027 phase should establish budget architecture, financial tracking, funding models and readiness for 2028 implementation.

Finance must support the vision without becoming reckless or fearful.

## Philosophy of the Workstream

Good stewardship is not merely spending less. It is achieving the right outcomes with what God has entrusted.

Money follows trust. Trust grows through clarity, accountability, transparency and measurable results.

## Core Responsibilities

- Design budget frameworks.
- Model restructuring and implementation costs.
- Develop diversified funding models.
- Create financial tracking systems.
- Coordinate budget requirements across workstreams.
- Design financial reporting templates.
- Establish campus funding checklists.
- Support donor and partner reporting.
- Develop sustainability assumptions for 2028 and beyond.

## Key Deliverables

### 2026

- Budget Framework
- Funding Model
- Financial Reporting Template
- Campus Funding Checklist
- Vision Economics Model

### 2027

- Approved Implementation Budget
- Active Tracking System
- Funding Pipeline Report
- Monthly Reporting Rhythm
- 2028 Funding Readiness Assessment

## Success Profile

The ideal lead is a financial steward, not merely a bookkeeper.

They combine precision with vision. They can explain numbers clearly to non-financial leaders. They see finance as a ministry enabler.

## Recruitment Profile

Look for someone who has:

- strong financial management experience;
- budgeting and reporting competence;
- non-profit or ministry finance exposure;
- financial modelling ability;
- ability to communicate clearly;
- donor reporting awareness;
- strategic judgement;
- integrity that inspires trust.

## Who Should Not Be Appointed

Avoid someone who:

- sees finance only as bookkeeping;
- cannot explain numbers simply;
- uses finance to block ministry unnecessarily;
- avoids innovation;
- hides bad news;
- confuses cost-cutting with stewardship.

## First 90 Days

Month One: understand current finances, funding streams and reporting practices.

Month Two: design the budget framework and identify major financial assumptions.

Month Three: draft the funding model, tracking approach and first financial dashboard requirements.

## Working Relationships

Reports to: Vision 2040 Executive Leadership.

Collaborates with: all workstreams, PMO, Data & Reporting, Campus Expansion, Communications and internal finance.

## Decision Authority

May design budget methodology, reporting formats and tracking systems.

Recommends budgets, funding models and major expenditure assumptions.

Requires approval for budget adoption, major commitments and funding policies.

## Team Structure

Recommended team:

- Finance Workstream Lead
- Accountant
- Budget Analyst
- Fundraising Specialist
- Grant Writer
- Data Analyst
- PMO Representative

## Success Indicators

The workstream is succeeding when:

- leadership trusts financial reports;
- budget discussions become strategic;
- major initiatives have sustainability models;
- financial surprises reduce;
- donor confidence increases;
- funding decisions are supported by clear evidence.

## Common Mistakes

- Treating funding as fundraising only.
- Ignoring operating costs after launch.
- Reporting too late for decisions.
- Failing to separate capital and recurring costs.
- Avoiding scenario planning.

## Advice from the PMO

You are not protecting money. You are protecting ministry.

Every Naira represents someone's sacrifice. Spend carefully, report truthfully and invest with faith and wisdom.

## Vision 2040 Goals Supported

This workstream supports all Vision 2040 goals by ensuring the financial capacity, credibility and sustainability required to execute them.

## Legacy

The Workstream Lead should leave behind budget frameworks, financial models, reporting systems, funding checklists, sustainability assumptions and donor confidence tools.

## Letter to My Successor

If you are reading this, remember that finance is a trust.

Do not become afraid of numbers. Do not hide behind them either. Use financial clarity to help leaders make wise decisions and keep the mission moving.
