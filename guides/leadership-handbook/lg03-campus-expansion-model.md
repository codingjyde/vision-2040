# Leadership Guide 03: Campus Expansion Model

## Purpose of this Guide

This guide defines the role of the leader responsible for designing Rhema Nigeria's repeatable campus expansion model.

## Why This Workstream Exists

Vision 2040 includes the establishment of Rhema campuses across Nigeria. That cannot be done safely by improvising each launch.

This workstream exists to make campus expansion repeatable, measurable and faithful to Rhema DNA.

## Mission Statement

Design, document and validate a campus multiplication system that enables Rhema Nigeria to launch and sustain healthy campuses from 2028 onwards.

## Strategic Context

The 2026-2027 phase is for model design and validation. No campus should be launched until the model has been tested.

This workstream prepares the practical engine for future expansion.

## Philosophy of the Workstream

Do not design a hero model.

Design a system ordinary faithful leaders can operate well.

The goal is not one impressive launch. The goal is a campus multiplication system that improves with each use.

## Core Responsibilities

- Design the campus operating model.
- Define location selection criteria.
- Develop staffing and role requirements.
- Create the Campus Launch Checklist.
- Create weekly and monthly operations checklists.
- Model financial sustainability.
- Pilot the launch model through simulation or limited validation.
- Capture lessons learned for future campus leaders.

## Key Deliverables

### 2026

- Campus Operating Model
- Location Selection Framework
- Staffing Model
- Financial Assumptions
- Draft Campus Launch Checklist

### 2027

- Validated Launch Checklist
- Weekly Operations Checklist
- Monthly Performance Checklist
- Pilot Report
- Campus Playbook
- 2028 Launch Readiness Package

## Success Profile

The ideal lead builds systems, not spectacles.

They understand operations, finance, ministry standards and people. They can resist premature expansion pressure. They are patient enough to validate before scaling.

## Recruitment Profile

Look for someone who has:

- opened or stabilised ministries, campuses or organisations;
- strong operational judgement;
- experience with multi-site work;
- ability to document processes;
- financial awareness;
- respect for academic and ministry standards;
- patience and courage.

## Who Should Not Be Appointed

Avoid someone who:

- loves launching but dislikes maintenance;
- improvises everything;
- thinks every campus must be unique;
- rushes ahead of readiness gates;
- measures success only by number of campuses opened.

## First 90 Days

Month One: study existing campuses and international Rhema launch patterns.

Month Two: map the campus lifecycle from exploration to multiplication.

Month Three: draft the operating model, launch checklist and validation plan.

## Working Relationships

Reports to: Vision 2040 Executive Leadership.

Collaborates with: Training, Finance, Data, Organisation Design, Publications and Communications.

Key dependency: Governance must define launch approval gates.

## Decision Authority

May design campus model options, checklists and pilot methodology.

Recommends campus adoption criteria and financial assumptions.

Requires approval for launch gates and final model adoption.

## Team Structure

Recommended team:

- Campus Expansion Lead
- Experienced Campus Director
- Operations Specialist
- Finance Representative
- Training Representative
- Data Representative
- Facilities Adviser
- Technology Representative

## Success Indicators

The workstream is succeeding when:

- a prospective Campus Director knows exactly what is required;
- common launch risks are identified early;
- every new campus can follow the same clear pathway;
- financial sustainability is tested before launch;
- the model improves through evidence.

## Common Mistakes

- Copying the first successful campus rather than extracting principles.
- Measuring launches instead of healthy campuses.
- Over-engineering documents.
- Ignoring what fails in years two and three.
- Treating local adaptation as compromise.

## Advice from the PMO

Your job is not to open campuses. Your job is to make campus expansion safe, repeatable and faithful.

Every campus should teach the next campus how to do better.

## Vision 2040 Goals Supported

This workstream directly supports the goal of establishing campuses across Nigeria and supports the training of leaders, church planting, missions, publications, alumni engagement and property planning.

## Legacy

The Workstream Lead should leave behind a Campus Playbook, launch checklists, operating standards, financial models, maturity framework and pilot evidence.

## Letter to My Successor

If you are reading this, protect the model from impatience.

Expansion is exciting, but premature expansion is expensive. Keep the gates clear. Keep the standards faithful. Keep learning from each launch.
