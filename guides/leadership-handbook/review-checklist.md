# Leadership Handbook Review Checklist

Owner: Vision 2040 PMO

Status: Draft

Version: 0.1.0

## Purpose

This checklist is used before any Leadership Guide moves from Draft to Review or Approved status.

It protects the handbook from becoming a set of attractive documents that are not grounded in the approved Vision 2040 source material.

## Use

Apply this checklist to each guide before approval.

Do not approve a guide until every applicable item has been addressed or recorded as an open question.

## 1. Source Alignment

- The guide uses the official workstream name.
- Any richer working name is clearly identified as a working name, not the approved name.
- The guide matches the approved purpose of the workstream.
- The guide reflects the approved scope of the workstream.
- The guide reflects the approved 2026 and 2027 deliverables.
- The guide does not introduce organisational facts not present in the source documents unless clearly marked as an assumption.
- The guide links to the relevant source documents.

## 2. Vision 2040 Alignment

- The guide explains how the role supports Vision 2040.
- The guide avoids treating the workstream as a permanent department.
- The guide recognises the 2026-2027 phase as a restructuring and validation phase.
- The guide reinforces the principle: build before you scale.
- The guide shows how the workstream builds enduring organisational capability.

## 3. Role Clarity

- The purpose of the role is clear.
- The mission statement is specific.
- Core responsibilities are practical and reviewable.
- Strategic responsibilities explain what the leader must build, protect, or change.
- Decision authority is clear.
- Approval boundaries are clear.
- Working relationships are clear.

## 4. Recruitment Quality

- The recruitment profile goes beyond qualifications.
- Character, judgement and ministry maturity are addressed.
- Technical competence is addressed where required.
- The guide explains who should not be appointed.
- The guide protects against predictable appointment mistakes.

## 5. KPI Alignment

- The KPI scorecard is checked against the approved Workstream JD & KPI Framework.
- KPI names are consistent with source material.
- Targets are consistent with source material.
- Frequency and weighting are included where available.
- Any new KPI is marked as proposed.

## 6. Practical Usefulness

- The guide can be used for recruitment.
- The guide can be used for onboarding.
- The guide can be used for coaching.
- The guide can be used for performance review.
- The guide can be used for succession planning.
- The guide would help a real workstream lead do the job better.

## 7. Knowledge Stewardship

- Reusable principles are linked rather than copied excessively.
- Definitions are not duplicated unnecessarily.
- Related guides are cross-referenced where useful.
- Assumptions and open questions are recorded.
- The guide leaves behind institutional memory.

## 8. Editorial Quality

- British English is used.
- Paragraphs are short.
- Active voice is used.
- Corporate jargon is avoided.
- The document avoids em dashes.
- The tone is clear, serious, warm and practical.

## Approval Note

A Leadership Guide should not be approved merely because it is well written.

It should be approved because it is faithful to the source material, useful to the workstream lead, and strong enough to preserve knowledge beyond the current restructuring phase.
