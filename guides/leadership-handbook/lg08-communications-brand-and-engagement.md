# Leadership Guide 08: Communications, Brand & Engagement

## Purpose of this Guide

This guide defines the role of the leader responsible for designing the communication and engagement architecture for Vision 2040.

## Why This Workstream Exists

Vision 2040 will not succeed merely because it is documented. People must understand it, believe it, carry it and participate in it.

This workstream shapes shared understanding and trust.

## Mission Statement

Design the messaging, brand, storytelling, internal communication and engagement systems that help stakeholders understand Vision 2040 and participate with confidence.

## Strategic Context

The 2026-2027 restructuring phase will create uncertainty. Clear communication reduces confusion, builds trust and sustains alignment.

## Philosophy of the Workstream

Communication is leadership.

The goal is not to publish more content. The goal is to shape understanding, confidence and participation.

Every leader communicates. This workstream designs the system that helps them communicate well.

## Core Responsibilities

- Develop the Vision 2040 messaging framework.
- Define audience segments.
- Create content and campaign checklists.
- Design internal communication rhythms.
- Establish a publishing calendar.
- Build story pipelines.
- Monitor audience engagement.
- Prepare crisis communication protocols.

## Key Deliverables

### 2026

- Messaging Framework
- Audience Segmentation
- Content Pipeline
- Internal Communication Plan
- Draft Story Framework

### 2027

- Active Communication Calendar
- Validated Content Checklist
- Campaign Checklist
- Weekly Publishing Checklist
- Crisis Communication Protocol
- Engagement Measurement Framework

## Success Profile

The ideal lead is a strategic storyteller.

They write clearly, understand people, protect brand consistency and know how to help leaders communicate without becoming artificial.

## Recruitment Profile

Look for someone who has:

- communications strategy experience;
- strong writing ability;
- storytelling skill;
- ministry sensitivity;
- brand discipline;
- ability to coach leaders;
- comfort with multiple channels;
- calm judgement during pressure.

## Who Should Not Be Appointed

Avoid someone who:

- confuses communication with graphics;
- chases trends constantly;
- measures success only by likes;
- communicates activities without outcomes;
- avoids internal communication;
- cannot write clearly.

## First 90 Days

Month One: understand audiences, current channels and existing messaging.

Month Two: draft the messaging framework and audience journeys.

Month Three: define content pipeline, internal communication rhythm and story capture process.

## Working Relationships

Reports to: Vision 2040 Executive Leadership.

Collaborates with: all workstreams, PMO, Publications, Data and Executive Leadership.

Every workstream should produce stories, updates and proof points through this workstream.

## Decision Authority

May design messaging frameworks, channel plans, content checklists and calendars.

Recommends brand positioning and campaign strategy.

Requires approval for major public campaigns and crisis messages.

## Team Structure

Recommended team:

- Communications Lead
- Writer or Editor
- Brand Manager
- Content Planner
- Video Storyteller
- Graphic Designer
- Social Media Manager
- Internal Communications Representative

## Success Indicators

The workstream is succeeding when:

- people can explain Vision 2040 clearly;
- partners feel informed and connected;
- internal rumours reduce;
- stories make progress tangible;
- content quality is consistent;
- leaders communicate with greater confidence.

## Common Mistakes

- Communicating activities instead of outcomes.
- Only communicating success.
- Becoming reactive.
- Ignoring internal audiences.
- Allowing inconsistent voice across channels.

## Advice from the PMO

Your work is not to fill social media. Your work is to help people understand and carry the vision.

Communication builds trust before trust is needed.

## Vision 2040 Goals Supported

This workstream supports all Vision 2040 goals by building alignment, confidence, participation and stakeholder engagement.

It especially supports student recruitment, partnership, alumni engagement, publications, media and funding confidence.

## Legacy

The Workstream Lead should leave behind a messaging framework, narrative framework, communication calendar, content checklists, internal communication system, crisis protocol and story archive.

## Letter to My Successor

If you are reading this, remember that communication is not noise. It is ministry leadership.

Tell the truth. Make the vision plain. Help people see where they fit. Build trust before you ask for commitment.
