# Vision 2040 Leadership Handbook

Owner: Vision 2040 PMO

Status: Draft

Version: 1.1.0

## Purpose

The Vision 2040 Leadership Handbook contains leadership guides for the nine workstream leads responsible for the 2026-2027 restructuring phase.

These guides are designed to help Rhema Nigeria recruit, appoint, onboard, coach, evaluate and eventually replace workstream leaders.

They are not ordinary job descriptions.

They are practical leadership documents for people entrusted with building enduring organisational capability.

## Editorial Note

Each guide should be specific to its workstream while remaining consistent with the handbook structure.

Avoid repeating the same Vision 2040 background in every guide.

Reference source documents instead.

A guide should not move to Approved status until it has been reviewed against the review checklist and checked against the KPI framework summary.
