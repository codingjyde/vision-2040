# Leadership Guide 02: Organisation Design & Capability

## Purpose of this Guide

This guide defines the expectations for the leader appointed to Workstream 02, Organisation Structure. The richer working name is Organisation Design & Capability.

The role exists to help Rhema Nigeria design the organisational architecture required for Vision 2040.

## Why This Workstream Exists

Vision 2040 cannot be carried by today's informal structures alone. The organisation must be deliberately designed for multiplication.

This workstream answers one question:

What kind of organisation must Rhema Nigeria become to fulfil Vision 2040?

## Mission Statement

Design a clear, capable and adaptable organisational structure that aligns roles, reporting lines, competencies, authority and performance expectations with the Vision 2040 mandate.

## Strategic Context

The 2026-2027 restructuring phase is not the time to add permanent departments quickly. It is the time to understand the capabilities Rhema Nigeria must build before 2028.

This workstream converts the Vision into roles, responsibilities, reporting lines, capability maps and performance scorecards.

## Philosophy of the Workstream

Structure follows mission.

Do not begin with departments. Begin with capabilities.

Vision 2040 requires capabilities such as campus multiplication, leadership development, missions deployment, alumni care, finance, data, media, translation, publishing and governance.

The organisation chart should serve those capabilities, not the other way round.

## Core Responsibilities

The Workstream Lead is responsible for:

- conducting an organisational diagnostic;
- defining current-state and future-state structures;
- mapping required organisational capabilities;
- defining roles, reporting lines and authority;
- creating role definition checklists;
- designing hiring readiness checklists;
- performing capability gap analysis;
- designing performance scorecard architecture;
- supporting transition planning for 2028.

## Key Deliverables

### 2026

- Current-State Diagnostic
- Target Structure
- Capability Framework
- Reporting Line Proposal
- Role Definition Format
- Initial Performance Scorecard Model

### 2027

- Capability Gap Closure Plan
- Hiring Readiness Checklist
- Performance Review Checklist
- Final Target Structure
- Transition Readiness Report
- 2028 Staffing Handover Package

## Success Profile

The ideal Workstream Lead understands organisations as living systems.

They do not merely draw boxes. They create clarity.

They know that authority and accountability must travel together. They can balance present realities with future demands.

## Recruitment Profile

Look for someone with:

- experience in organisational design;
- strong leadership judgement;
- understanding of ministry culture;
- ability to map roles and capabilities;
- HR or performance management exposure;
- ability to listen across departments;
- courage to clarify reporting lines;
- practical wisdom, not just theory.

## Who Should Not Be Appointed

Avoid appointing someone who:

- loves hierarchy for its own sake;
- creates unnecessary management layers;
- designs around personalities rather than mission;
- confuses titles with responsibility;
- avoids difficult conversations about accountability;
- measures progress by headcount.

## First 90 Days

Month One: listen, diagnose and gather current organisational data.

Month Two: map capabilities and identify structural tensions.

Month Three: draft the target structure, role framework and capability gap analysis.

## Working Relationships

Reports to: Vision 2040 Executive Leadership.

Collaborates with: PMO, Campus Expansion, Training, Finance, HR and Operations.

Depends on: Governance & Leadership for decision rights, and Data & Reporting for performance metrics.

## Decision Authority

May design role frameworks, structure options, checklist templates and scorecard formats.

Recommends target structure, reporting lines and capability priorities.

Requires approval for final organisational design and major role changes.

## Team Structure

Recommended team:

- Organisation Design Lead
- HR Specialist
- Operations Representative
- Finance Representative
- Campus Representative
- PMO Representative

## Success Indicators

The workstream is succeeding when:

- leaders understand who owns what;
- role confusion reduces;
- hiring conversations become clearer;
- performance expectations become measurable;
- departments collaborate without territorial behaviour;
- future capability gaps are visible before they become crises.

## Common Mistakes

- Designing around current personalities.
- Adding layers instead of improving clarity.
- Ignoring informal influence.
- Separating accountability from authority.
- Creating structures that cannot scale.

## Advice from the PMO

Your work is not to make the chart look impressive. Your work is to make responsibility clear.

A good structure helps people serve better. A poor structure multiplies confusion.

## Vision 2040 Goals Supported

This workstream supports all goals by ensuring the organisation has the structure and capability to execute them.

It especially supports campus expansion, leadership training, alumni care, missions, publications, media, RMAI and long-term institutional resilience.

## Legacy

The Workstream Lead should leave behind a capability framework, role architecture, target structure, performance scorecard model, hiring readiness tools and a 2028 transition plan.

## Letter to My Successor

If you are reading this, remember that structure is not about control. It is about service.

Do not protect boxes. Protect clarity.

Keep asking what the mission requires. Build an organisation that can carry the vision without exhausting the people.
