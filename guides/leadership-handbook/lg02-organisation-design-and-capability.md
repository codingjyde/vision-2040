## Purpose of this Guide

This guide defines the leadership expectations, responsibilities, operating philosophy and success profile for the person appointed to lead Workstream 02, Organisation Structure.

The official workstream name is Organisation Structure. The working name, Organisation Design & Capability, is used in this guide to clarify the deeper purpose of the assignment.

The role exists to help Rhema Nigeria design the organisational architecture required for Vision 2040.

It supports recruitment, appointment, onboarding, coaching, performance review, succession planning and knowledge transfer.

This is not a conventional job description. It is a leadership guide.

## Why This Workstream Exists

Vision 2040 cannot be carried by today's informal structures alone.

The organisation must be deliberately designed for multiplication.

This workstream answers one question:

What kind of organisation must Rhema Nigeria become to fulfil Vision 2040?

It exists to translate the Vision into roles, reporting lines, accountability, competency requirements, capability gaps and performance expectations.

It must help Rhema Nigeria avoid two opposite errors.

The first error is remaining too informal for the size of the assignment.

The second error is building a heavy structure before the organisation is ready to carry it.

Good organisation design serves the mission without suffocating the ministry.

## Mission Statement

Design a clear, capable and adaptable organisational structure that aligns roles, reporting lines, competencies, authority and performance expectations with the Vision 2040 mandate.

## Strategic Context

The 2026-2027 restructuring phase is not the time to add permanent departments quickly.

It is the time to understand the capabilities Rhema Nigeria must build before 2028.

This workstream converts the Vision into organisational architecture.

It defines:

- the target structure;
- the role architecture;
- the reporting relationships;
- the capability requirements;
- the performance scorecard model;
- the hiring readiness requirements;
- the transition path into 2028 implementation.

This workstream is closely dependent on Workstream 01, Governance & Leadership, because role responsibility must align with decision rights.

It also depends on Workstream 07, Data & Reporting, because performance scorecards require reliable indicators.

It must collaborate with every other workstream because each workstream represents a future organisational capability.

## Philosophy of the Workstream

Structure follows mission.

Do not begin with departments.

Begin with capabilities.

Vision 2040 requires capabilities such as campus multiplication, leadership development, missions deployment, alumni care, finance, data, media, translation, publishing, property stewardship, governance and partner engagement.

The organisation chart should serve those capabilities, not the other way round.

A good structure answers practical questions:

- Who owns this work?
- Who has authority to decide?
- Who must be consulted?
- Who must be informed?
- What capability does this role strengthen?
- What happens if the current person leaves?

This workstream should resist designing around personalities.

People are important.

But the structure must serve the mission beyond any one individual.

## Core Responsibilities

The Workstream Lead is responsible for:

- conducting an organisational diagnostic;
- establishing a current-state baseline;
- designing the target organisational structure;
- defining roles, reporting lines and accountability;
- creating a capability framework;
- developing role definition checklists;
- developing hiring readiness checklists;
- conducting capability gap analysis;
- creating gap closure action plans;
- designing cascading performance scorecards;
- creating a performance review checklist template;
- preparing a 2028 transition readiness package.

## Strategic Responsibilities

Beyond the practical deliverables, the Workstream Lead must help Rhema Nigeria think clearly about organisational capacity.

This leader must:

- shift discussion from departments to capabilities;
- ensure authority and accountability stay connected;
- identify capability gaps before expansion exposes them;
- prevent structure from becoming a political tool;
- help leaders distinguish current convenience from future readiness;
- design for succession, not only present occupancy;
- ensure future roles can be recruited, developed and assessed.

The deeper work is not drawing an organogram.

The deeper work is building clarity that can carry growth.

## Success Profile

The ideal Workstream Lead understands organisations as living systems.

They do not merely draw boxes.

They create clarity.

They know that authority and accountability must travel together.

They can balance present realities with future demands.

They are not impressed by complexity for its own sake.

They ask simple questions that expose hidden confusion.

They know when a problem is structural, when it is relational, and when it is a leadership issue.

They are able to listen widely without becoming captured by departmental interests.

They can help leaders accept necessary clarity without turning the process into a power struggle.

## Who Should Not Be Appointed

Avoid appointing someone who:

- loves hierarchy for its own sake;
- creates unnecessary management layers;
- designs around personalities rather than mission;
- confuses titles with responsibility;
- avoids difficult conversations about accountability;
- measures progress by headcount;
- treats structure as a status symbol;
- uses role design to reward loyalty rather than serve mission;
- cannot separate current relationships from future requirements.

## Common Mistakes

- Designing around current personalities.
- Adding layers instead of improving clarity.
- Ignoring informal influence.
- Separating accountability from authority.
- Creating structures that cannot scale.
- Assuming every capability requires a new department.
- Treating volunteers, staff and contractors as if they need identical management systems.
- Designing a 2040 structure without defining a 2028 transition path.

## Advice from the PMO

Your work is not to make the chart look impressive.

Your work is to make responsibility clear.

A good structure helps people serve better.

A poor structure multiplies confusion.

Do not ask first, "What department should own this?"

Ask first, "What capability must exist, and what role must carry it?"

Do not design around the strongest personalities in the room.

Design around the mission.

The organisation chart should help the next leader understand the work quickly.

If a new person cannot read the structure and understand how responsibility flows, the structure is not clear enough.

## Assumptions and Open Questions

### Assumptions

- The official workstream name remains Organisation Structure, while Organisation Design & Capability may be used as a clarifying working name.
- The target structure should include phased structures, not only one end-state organogram.
- Capability mapping should precede permanent departmental decisions.
- Final authority for organisational design remains with Executive Leadership.

### Open Questions

- Should Rhema Nigeria maintain separate 2028, mid-scale and 2040 structure models?
- Which roles should be treated as national roles, regional roles, campus roles, volunteer roles or contractor roles?
- How will succession planning be embedded into each critical role?
- How will informal influence be recognised without creating unofficial authority structures?
- What HR systems will be required to support the approved structure?

### Decisions Required

- Approve the use of a capability framework as the foundation for structure design.
- Decide whether the 2028 target structure and 2040 target structure should be separate documents.
- Decide which roles are mission-critical before 2028 implementation begins.
- Decide how performance scorecards will connect to annual reviews.

## Letter to My Successor

If you are reading this, remember that structure is not about control.

It is about service.

Do not protect boxes.

Protect clarity.

Keep asking what the mission requires.

Some people will want structure to protect what already exists.

Others will want structure to prove that the organisation is growing.

Resist both pressures.

Design for the assignment, not for appearances.

Authority without responsibility creates frustration.

Responsibility without authority creates exhaustion.

Keep them together.

Build an organisation that can carry the vision without crushing the people.

That is your stewardship.
