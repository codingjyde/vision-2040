# Leadership Guide 05: AfterGrad, Missions & Alumni

## Purpose of this Guide

This guide defines the role of the leader responsible for designing the post-graduation ecosystem for Rhema Nigeria.

## Why This Workstream Exists

Most schools measure success at graduation. Vision 2040 must measure success after graduation.

This workstream asks what happened because a person came to Rhema.

## Mission Statement

Design a graduate pathway system that supports alumni into ministry, church planting, missions, societal influence, supportive leadership and lifelong connection with Rhema Nigeria.

## Strategic Context

Vision 2040 includes church planters, missionaries, societal leaders, supportive leaders and a strong relational alumni association.

This workstream converts graduation into commissioning.

## Philosophy of the Workstream

Graduation is not the finish line. It is a handoff.

The goal is not simply to maintain a database. The goal is to help graduates keep moving towards God's purpose.

## Core Responsibilities

- Map graduate pathways.
- Design church planting support systems.
- Create missionary deployment readiness criteria.
- Design missionary care and re-entry protocols.
- Build alumni engagement systems.
- Develop graduate outcome tracking.
- Create placement and mentoring structures.
- Feed graduate experience back into Training, Campus Expansion and Data.

## Key Deliverables

### 2026

- Graduate Pathway Map
- Draft Church Planting Checklist
- Draft Missionary Deployment Checklist
- Alumni Segmentation Model
- Graduate Data Requirements

### 2027

- Validated Church Planting Checklist
- Missionary Support Framework
- Alumni Engagement Checklist
- Graduate Impact Measurement System
- Graduate Success Framework

## Success Profile

The ideal lead loves people and systems.

They remember names, build relationships and think strategically. They understand ministry deployment, missions, alumni care and the long-term fruit of training.

## Recruitment Profile

Look for someone who has:

- strong relational ability;
- pastoral sensitivity;
- missions or deployment experience;
- alumni or community-building experience;
- strategic thinking;
- follow-through;
- ability to connect graduates with opportunities;
- respect for different callings.

## Who Should Not Be Appointed

Avoid someone who:

- sees alumni mainly as donors;
- prefers systems over people;
- disappears into administration;
- treats all graduates the same;
- measures success only by event attendance;
- fails to follow up.

## First 90 Days

Month One: understand graduate records, existing alumni relationships and deployment stories.

Month Two: segment graduate pathways and define early outcome measures.

Month Three: draft church planting, missions and alumni engagement frameworks.

## Working Relationships

Reports to: Vision 2040 Executive Leadership.

Collaborates with: Training, Campus Expansion, Publications, Finance, Communications and Data.

External partners may include churches, mission agencies, pastors, denominations and alumni leaders.

## Decision Authority

May design pathway frameworks, engagement checklists and deployment tools.

Recommends deployment policies, alumni programme structure and support models.

Requires approval for formal deployment policies and major alumni initiatives.

## Team Structure

Recommended team:

- AfterGrad Lead
- Alumni Coordinator
- Missions Representative
- Church Planting Representative
- Graduate Data Lead
- Member Care Representative
- Communications Representative

## Success Indicators

The workstream is succeeding when:

- graduates feel remembered and supported;
- churches request Rhema graduates;
- mission placements are healthier;
- alumni mentor younger graduates;
- graduate stories inform organisational learning;
- Rhema can locate and understand its graduate impact.

## Common Mistakes

- Losing contact after graduation.
- Treating all graduates alike.
- Becoming event-driven.
- Measuring newsletters instead of fruit.
- Underestimating missionary care.

## Advice from the PMO

People are not projects.

Your work is to keep graduates connected to purpose, support and opportunity.

## Vision 2040 Goals Supported

This workstream directly supports church planting, missions, societal leadership, supportive leadership, alumni association, RMAI and training outcomes.

## Legacy

The Workstream Lead should leave behind graduate pathways, alumni systems, church planting and missionary readiness tools, care protocols, placement systems and a graduate impact framework.

## Letter to My Successor

If you are reading this, never let the database replace the relationship.

Serve alumni first. Listen to their stories. Learn from their struggles. Help each graduate keep taking the next faithful step.
