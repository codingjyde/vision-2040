# Leadership Guide 07: Data, Insights & Reporting

## Purpose of this Guide

This guide defines the role of the leader responsible for making Vision 2040 measurable, visible and learnable.

## Why This Workstream Exists

Without reliable data, Vision 2040 becomes a collection of intentions.

With reliable data, leadership can see progress, identify risk, make better decisions and learn faster than the organisation grows.

## Mission Statement

Design the measurement, reporting, dashboard and organisational learning systems that enable evidence-based leadership throughout Vision 2040.

## Strategic Context

The 2026-2027 restructuring phase must produce standardised reporting, validated KPIs, live dashboards and data quality practices before implementation begins.

## Philosophy of the Workstream

Data exists to improve ministry.

The goal is not more reports. The goal is better decisions.

Every report should answer, "What decision does this help?"

## Core Responsibilities

- Design KPI frameworks for all workstreams.
- Define data sources, flows, ownership and storage.
- Create data capture and reporting checklists.
- Build dashboard specifications.
- Establish data quality standards.
- Train Workstream Leads on reporting expectations.
- Capture lessons learned.
- Create the Vision 2040 Measurement Framework.

## Key Deliverables

### 2026

- KPI Framework
- Data Architecture
- Reporting Standards
- Data Dictionary
- Dashboard Specification

### 2027

- Live Executive Dashboard
- Reporting Validation
- Data Quality Review Process
- Workstream Reporting Training
- Organisational Learning Report

## Success Profile

The ideal lead is curious, accurate and practical.

They notice patterns. They ask better questions. They can explain data to busy leaders without overwhelming them.

## Recruitment Profile

Look for someone who has:

- performance measurement experience;
- dashboard or reporting experience;
- data quality awareness;
- ability to translate data into decisions;
- clear communication;
- patience with imperfect systems;
- respect for both numbers and stories.

## Who Should Not Be Appointed

Avoid someone who:

- collects data for its own sake;
- overwhelms leaders with reports;
- believes more metrics always help;
- hides uncertainty;
- cannot explain numbers plainly;
- ignores qualitative learning.

## First 90 Days

Month One: identify workstream decisions and required data.

Month Two: define KPI architecture, data owners and reporting rhythm.

Month Three: draft dashboard requirements, data dictionary and quality standards.

## Working Relationships

Reports to: Vision 2040 Executive Leadership.

Collaborates with: all workstreams, PMO, IT and Finance.

Every workstream is both a data producer and a data user.

## Decision Authority

May design KPI methodology, reporting formats, dashboard specifications and data standards.

Recommends KPI adoption and data governance policies.

Requires approval for executive dashboard design and major KPI changes.

## Team Structure

Recommended team:

- Data Workstream Lead
- Business Analyst
- Dashboard Developer
- Data Quality Lead
- IT Representative
- PMO Representative
- Workstream Data Representatives

## Success Indicators

The workstream is succeeding when:

- leaders trust the numbers;
- meetings start with evidence;
- reporting becomes shorter and clearer;
- risks are identified earlier;
- data quality improves;
- lessons learned are captured and reused.

## Common Mistakes

- Measuring activity instead of outcomes.
- Producing reports nobody reads.
- Using undefined terms.
- Ignoring leading indicators.
- Treating stories as less valuable than numbers.

## Advice from the PMO

You are not building dashboards. You are building confidence.

Bad news delivered early is a gift. Good data helps leaders act before problems harden.

## Vision 2040 Goals Supported

This workstream supports every Vision 2040 goal by measuring progress, quality, outcomes and learning.

## Legacy

The Workstream Lead should leave behind a measurement framework, data dictionary, dashboard, reporting standards, quality process and institutional learning rhythm.

## Letter to My Successor

If you are reading this, remember that data is stewardship.

Never manipulate numbers to protect appearances. Tell the truth clearly. Help leaders see what matters and act with wisdom.
