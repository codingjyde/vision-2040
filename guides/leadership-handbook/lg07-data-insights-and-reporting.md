This guide defines the expectations for the person appointed to lead Workstream 07, Data & Reporting.

The guide should support recruitment, appointment, onboarding, coaching, performance review, succession planning and knowledge transfer.

## Why This Workstream Exists

Without reliable data, Vision 2040 can become a collection of intentions.

With reliable data, leadership can see progress, identify risk, make better decisions and learn faster than the organisation grows.

This workstream exists to build the measurement and reporting ecosystem that supports evidence-based leadership. It turns Vision 2040 from aspiration into something that can be tracked, tested, improved and governed.

## Mission Statement

Design and validate the measurement, reporting, dashboard and organisational learning systems that enable Rhema Nigeria to lead Vision 2040 with evidence, clarity, accountability and wisdom.

## Strategic Context

The 2026-2027 restructuring phase must produce standardised reporting, validated KPIs, live dashboards and data quality practices before implementation begins.

This workstream serves every other workstream. Each workstream produces data. Each workstream also needs data to make better decisions.

For that reason, Data & Reporting should not operate as an isolated technical function. It should become the nervous system of the Vision 2040 programme, helping leadership sense what is happening across governance, structure, campuses, training, graduates, finance, communications and publications.

## Philosophy of the Workstream

Data exists to improve ministry.

The goal is not more reports. The goal is better decisions.

Every report should answer one question:

What decision does this help?

If a report does not help a leader understand, decide, correct, invest, stop, improve or pray more intelligently, the report should be redesigned.

The workstream should measure outcomes, not merely activity. It should also preserve learning, not merely count events.

Numbers matter.

Stories matter too.

A faithful data system should combine both.

## Core Responsibilities

The Workstream Lead is responsible for:

- designing KPI frameworks for all workstreams;
- defining data sources, flows, ownership, storage and access;
- creating data capture and reporting checklists;
- building executive dashboard specifications;
- establishing data quality standards and validation protocols;
- training Workstream Leads on reporting expectations;
- defining a Vision 2040 data dictionary;
- identifying leading and lagging indicators;
- creating a reporting rhythm that supports PMO and executive review cycles;
- capturing lessons learned from workstreams and pilots;
- supporting the development of institutional memory;
- creating the Vision 2040 Measurement Framework.

## Strategic Responsibilities

Beyond technical reporting, this leader must help Rhema Nigeria become a learning organisation.

They must build confidence in the numbers.

They must reduce confusion around definitions.

They must help leaders ask better questions.

They must ensure bad news is surfaced early enough to be useful.

They must protect data integrity when pressure rises to make results look better than they are.

This role requires both technical discipline and spiritual honesty.

## Success Profile

The ideal lead is curious, accurate and practical.

They notice patterns.

They ask better questions.

They can explain data to busy leaders without overwhelming them.

They understand that a dashboard is only useful if it helps a real leader make a real decision.

They are not intimidated by imperfect data, but they also refuse to normalise poor data quality.

## Recruitment Profile

### Essential Experience

Look for someone with:

- performance measurement experience;
- reporting or dashboard experience;
- data quality awareness;
- ability to translate data into decisions;
- clear communication;
- experience working across teams;
- strong documentation habits;
- patience with imperfect systems.

### Preferred Experience

Helpful experience includes:

- ministry or non-profit reporting;
- KPI framework design;
- dashboard design;
- monitoring and evaluation;
- business analysis;
- data governance;
- basic database or spreadsheet competence;
- organisational learning or knowledge management.

### Personal Qualities

Look for:

- curiosity;
- honesty;
- patience;
- accuracy;
- discretion;
- courage;
- humility;
- teachability;
- calmness under pressure;
- respect for both numbers and stories.

### Technical Competencies

The leader does not need to be the only technical person on the team, but they should understand:

- KPI design;
- indicator definitions;
- data quality controls;
- reporting cycles;
- dashboard requirements;
- data capture workflows;
- basic analytics;
- data privacy and access control.

## Who Should Not Be Appointed

Avoid appointing someone who:

- collects data for its own sake;
- overwhelms leaders with reports;
- believes more metrics always help;
- hides uncertainty;
- cannot explain numbers plainly;
- ignores qualitative learning;
- manipulates figures to avoid difficult conversations;
- treats dashboards as the final product;
- loses patience with non-technical leaders.

## Common Mistakes

- Measuring activity instead of outcomes.
- Producing reports nobody reads.
- Using undefined terms.
- Ignoring leading indicators.
- Treating stories as less valuable than numbers.
- Building dashboards before defining decisions.
- Allowing departments to use conflicting definitions.
- Reporting too late for correction.
- Treating data quality as an IT problem only.

## Advice from the PMO

You are not building dashboards.

You are building confidence.

Bad news delivered early is a gift.

Good data helps leaders act before problems harden.

Never manipulate numbers to protect appearances.

Never bury leaders under reports they cannot use.

Help Rhema Nigeria see clearly, decide wisely and learn faithfully.

## Assumptions and Open Questions

### Assumptions

- Each workstream will provide timely data according to agreed reporting rhythms.
- The PMO will enforce reporting discipline.
- The dashboard will be designed around executive decisions, not merely available data.
- Data capture can be embedded into existing workstream workflows.
- Qualitative learning will be treated as part of the measurement system.

### Open Questions

- What tools will be used for dashboarding, data storage and reporting?
- Who owns final data validation for each workstream?
- Which Vision 2040 indicators should be treated as leading indicators?
- What data privacy or access rules are required?
- How often should executive dashboards be refreshed?
- What minimum reporting capacity must every Workstream Lead possess?

### Decisions Required

- Approve the Vision 2040 data dictionary structure.
- Approve the first executive dashboard scope.
- Approve the reporting cadence for all workstreams.
- Assign data owners for each workstream.
- Decide which technology platform will support dashboarding and reporting.
