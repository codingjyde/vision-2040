## Purpose of this Guide

This guide defines the leadership expectations, responsibilities, operating philosophy and success profile for the person appointed to lead Workstream 01, Governance & Leadership.

It supports recruitment, appointment, onboarding, coaching, performance review, succession planning and knowledge transfer.

This is not a conventional job description. It is a leadership guide.

## Why This Workstream Exists

Vision 2040 is an organisational transformation programme. It is not simply a collection of projects.

Transformation requires clear governance, disciplined decision-making and consistent execution.

This workstream designs the operating framework that enables every other workstream to succeed.

Its responsibility is not to control the programme. Its responsibility is to create clarity.

## Mission Statement

Design and establish governance systems that enable Vision 2040 to move with clarity, accountability, speed and unity while preserving Rhema Nigeria's values, mission and long-term vision.

## Strategic Context

The 2026-2027 restructuring phase prepares Rhema Nigeria for sustainable expansion from 2028 onwards.

No campus should be opened merely because governance documents exist. Expansion should begin only after governance, operating models and organisational capability have been tested and validated.

This workstream establishes governance, decision rights, PMO operations, accountability, review cycles and programme discipline.

## Philosophy of the Workstream

Governance does not exist to create bureaucracy. Governance exists to remove uncertainty.

Good governance enables ministry. Poor governance slows ministry.

The purpose of governance is to help good people make good decisions consistently.

This workstream should keep asking:

- Does this reduce confusion?
- Does this clarify responsibility?
- Does this improve decision quality?
- Does this help leaders move faster?
- Does this preserve trust?

If the answer is no, the governance tool should be reconsidered.

## Core Responsibilities

The Workstream Lead is responsible for:

- designing the Vision 2040 governance model;
- defining decision rights across all workstreams;
- drafting and refining the PMO Charter;
- creating executive review cycles;
- designing escalation protocols;
- creating governance checklists and templates;
- maintaining governance documentation;
- supporting phase-gate readiness assessments;
- preserving strategic alignment across all workstreams.

## Strategic Responsibilities

Beyond producing governance documents, the Workstream Lead must build the habits that make governance work.

This includes:

- reducing decision latency;
- improving meeting quality;
- ensuring significant decisions are recorded;
- protecting the PMO from becoming bureaucratic;
- helping leaders distinguish ownership from involvement;
- ensuring the governance model can scale beyond the 2026-2027 phase.

## Success Profile

The ideal Workstream Lead is an organisational architect.

They think in systems. They simplify complexity. They help leaders make better decisions.

They are trusted because they are fair. They influence without relying on title. They enjoy building structures that help others succeed.

They measure success by organisational effectiveness rather than personal visibility.

## Recruitment Profile

### Essential Experience

Look for someone with:

- senior organisational leadership experience;
- governance or board exposure;
- programme leadership experience;
- strong judgement;
- ability to write clearly;
- ability to facilitate difficult conversations;
- ministry understanding;
- emotional maturity;
- courage to challenge respectfully;
- patience to build before scaling.

### Preferred Experience

Helpful experience includes:

- PMO design or implementation;
- organisational transformation;
- board or executive committee support;
- decision-rights design;
- change management;
- strategy execution.

### Personal Qualities

The person should be trusted, calm, disciplined, wise, discreet and direct without being harsh.

They must care more about the health of the programme than their own visibility.

### Technical Competencies

A professional project manager may serve strongly on the team, but the Workstream Lead should not be chosen only because they can manage tasks.

This role requires organisational judgement.

## Who Should Not Be Appointed

Avoid appointing someone who:

- enjoys bureaucracy;
- protects process more than people;
- needs positional authority to influence others;
- creates unnecessary documentation;
- cannot challenge senior leaders respectfully;
- dislikes collaboration;
- avoids accountability;
- confuses activity with progress.

## Common Mistakes

- Building bureaucracy instead of clarity.
- Confusing governance with control.
- Producing documents nobody uses.
- Becoming the bottleneck.
- Solving problems other workstreams should own.
- Allowing governance to become static.

## Advice from the PMO

You are not building paperwork. You are building confidence.

Every governance decision should make ministry easier. Every checklist should reduce uncertainty. Every meeting should produce clarity. Every document should help someone make a better decision.

The PMO succeeds when leaders say, "Things work better because governance is clear."

## Annual Leadership Rhythm

### Weekly

Review issues, support workstreams and monitor governance compliance.

### Monthly

Conduct governance review, PMO meeting, risk review and executive reporting.

### Quarterly

Audit governance, review decision effectiveness, update documentation and improve processes.

### Annually

Evaluate governance, record lessons learned, update the governance model and prepare transition documentation.

## Letter to My Successor

If you are reading this, leadership has entrusted this responsibility to you.

Remember that governance is not about control. It is about stewardship.

You will often be asked to solve problems quickly. Resist the temptation to create unnecessary complexity. Whenever possible, simplify.

Listen carefully before redesigning systems. Protect relationships as carefully as you protect processes. Never allow governance to become an obstacle to ministry.

Your greatest achievement will not be a beautifully written governance manual. It will be an organisation where leaders trust one another, decisions are made wisely, responsibilities are clear, and Vision 2040 continues to advance with unity, excellence and faithfulness.

Build systems that outlive you.
