# Vision 2040 Programme

Owner: Rhema Nigeria

Purpose: Organise documentation for the Vision 2040 restructuring and expansion programme.

Status: Initial scaffold

Vision 2040 is Rhema Nigeria's long-term programme context for sustainable national and international expansion.

The 2026-2027 phase is a restructuring phase focused on systems, governance, leadership, operating models, documentation, and institutional knowledge.
